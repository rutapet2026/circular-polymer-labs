PROTOCOL SPECIFICATION & PUBLIC AUDITOR

Verifiable Plastic Recovery Infrastructure.

RutaPET is an open, zero-server traceability protocol built by Circular Polymer Labs LLC. Field collection events across multi-polymer streams (PET, PP, PE) are cryptographically hashed, pinned to decentralized storage (IPFS / OORT), and anchored on-chain for transparent EPR settlement.

01 — EDGE CAPTURE

Multi-Polymer Field Proof

Mobile collector nodes capture timestamped video evidence, net mass (PET, PP, PE), and SHA-256 media digests locally before batching daily shift manifests.

02 — DECENTRALIZED STORAGE

Immutable IPFS Manifests

Each shift manifest (rutapet.jornada.v1) is serialized into canonical JSON, hashed, and pinned to IPFS/OORT Storage without centralized cloud databases.

03 — ON-CHAIN SETTLEMENT

Direct Collector Payouts

Manifest CIDs are anchored on public ledgers (Polygon / Solana), enabling auditable Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) accounting with high direct payout ratios.
